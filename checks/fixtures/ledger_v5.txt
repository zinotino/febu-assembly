WHAT THIS IS
  One record of what this group has found out about febu, in the group's voice. The group
  works on one question at a time, together. Nobody approves anything. Anyone can check
  anything. Nothing is removed except personal information (rule 6). An answer says only what
  its link shows.

THE RULES
  1. One question at a time. The group keeps one open question about febu, or about something
     febu touches. A question asks what a link anyone can open could settle. A forecast of
     what nobody here does goes in the chat, not here.
  2. Any member may propose a question in the chat. While no question is open, the first
     proposal to get "yes" from three members other than its proposer opens, on the day of
     its third yes. Proposals and yeses may be posted while a question is open; when it
     closes, the proposal that reached three yeses first opens that day. If none has, the
     next one to reach three opens.
  3. The answer is the group's. Anyone may post wording for the open question's answer: what
     it SAYS, LINKs that open for anyone without asking, and WRONG IF lines. Every part of SAYS
     needs a WRONG IF line that would catch it; if SAYS claims more, cut SAYS. A WRONG IF line
     about something that can still change ends on a stated date, so nobody can make it true
     later. A question may have several answers. The group's pages show each as the
     Assembly's; this file keeps who posted or changed each line, so that rule 4 can be
     checked.
  4. An answer closes HOLDS, or DOESN'T HOLD, when three members other than its posters have
     each posted the same result and what they Saw, and at least one of them opened every link
     in it by hand, with no script. If fewer than four active members are left once its
     posters are set aside, every one of them is needed, and never fewer than two. A line that
     covers many items (pages, payments, transactions) is tested only over all of them: every
     page, every payment, or the explorer's whole filtered list. A post on a sample is a SEEN
     item that counts toward nothing, unless it finds a wrong item, which counts toward
     DOESN'T HOLD. A question closes when one of its answers closes HOLDS. An answer that
     closes DOESN'T HOLD stays, marked, and the question stays open for another answer.
  5. An answer is kept open when two people, member or not, other than its posters, have
     posted the same result with one word, and two others the same result with the other
     word. It is reopened when, after it closed, two such people post the same result with
     the word it did not close on. Either happens on the day that second pair is complete,
     and from then on a close counts only SEEN items posted after that day. One person's
     different result is a SEEN item like any other and changes nothing on its own. A
     question not closed by the end of the 30th day after it opened is marked CAN'T TELL,
     and the next question opens (rule 2). Results may still be posted under it, and a close
     after that day stands, but neither it nor a reopen takes the slot back.
  6. Anyone may add to this file, through the chat and the weekly turn. Nothing is removed or
     rewritten: every change is a new dated line. Wrong answers stay, marked. Personal
     information may be replaced by "[removed]".
  7. Each week a different member checks both ways: every result, proposal, yes and vote
     posted in the chat that week is in this file, and every SEEN, PROPOSED and VOTES item in
     this file has its post in the chat. That member adds whatever was posted that week and is
     not yet in the file, and one LAST POSTS line, starting from the file whose SHA-256 was
     last posted, and posts the new file's SHA-256 in the chat and in one public place outside
     it, naming the place; the chat post sets the order. The ledger is the file that matches
     the last posted hash, wherever it is served. A turn not done by the end of its week
     passes to the next member in the order.
  8. A rule changes, and anything is said or done in the group's name beyond what rules 2 and
     4 settle, only after it has been posted in the chat for 7 days, at least four active
     members have written "yes" (every active member, if fewer than five are active, and
     never fewer than two), and at most one has written "object". An objection says which line is wrong and what would change the
     objector's mind. It is counted from the VOTES section alone, at the end of each day from
     the 7th day after the post, on the votes dated by then: each member's latest vote
     stands, and "active" means active that day. It passes on the first day the count holds.
     The old wording stays, word for word, in archive/. Anyone may copy this file and run it
     their own way.
  9. No money moves in the group's name: no pool, no treasury, no bonds, no pay, no new token.
     The only token named here is $FEBU, and nothing in the group's name states or implies a
     price for it. No totals, scores or rankings of members, here or on the group's pages.
  10. Anything only one person can do is a bug. It goes under KNOWN BUGS with a date to fix
      it by.

           gives "all" for each; for DOESN'T HOLD, they name at least one line in common.

  Number   proposals P1, P2 in the order posted; questions Q1, Q2 in the order they open;
           answers 1, 2 under each question in the order posted.
  Hash     the SHA-256 of ledger.txt. Download the file and hash the file; pasting the text into
           a box can change it. The ledger page shows the hash too.
  Nobody judges whether a question is worth asking: rule 2's three yeses are the only gate.

HOW TO CHECK AN ANSWER
  1. Open the link in the answer. You need no account.
  2. Look for what the answer says is there, before you read anyone else's post about it.
  3. Post in the chat: the question and answer number, what you saw (the number, the words or
     the date), which WRONG IF lines you tested (for a line over many items, how many you
     opened), one thing on the page that is not in the answer or in any earlier post about
     it, and your result. If a script did part of it, say whose script and where it ran. A
     post that only says HOLDS counts for nothing.
  4. The weekly turn copies your post under the answer as a SEEN item, in this form:
       - Name, YYYY-MM-DD: HOLDS on lines 1, 2. Items: line 2, all 164. Script: <command> by
         <who> via <where>. Saw: <what you saw>. New: <the one new thing>.
     Items and Script only when they apply.
  An answer is OPEN until rule 4 closes it. A reopened answer closes the same way, counting
  only posts made after it reopened.

HOW A QUESTION IS WRITTEN
  P n. Name, YYYY-MM-DD: the question, in one sentence.
       Yes: Name YYYY-MM-DD, Name YYYY-MM-DD, Name YYYY-MM-DD.
       Opened as Q n.   (added on the day it opens)

  Q n. (opened YYYY-MM-DD, from P m)
    ASKS        the question, as proposed
    ANSWER 1.   (who, YYYY-MM-DD)
      SAYS      one sentence
      LINK      full links you can tap
      WRONG IF  what would show it false
      SEEN      results, as they come
  WRONG IF is written before anyone checks. Until an answer's first SEEN item, anyone may
  change it with a dated CHANGED line that names who changed it; after that, never: post a new
  answer instead. NOTE lines may be added at any time, by anyone, and change nothing.

RISKS WE ACCEPT
  - Three members who agree in advance can close a false answer, until two people look.
  - Two people who agree in advance can keep every answer open until CAN'T TELL. Each time,
    that costs the group one question's 30 days.
  - Nobody is paid to check, nothing makes anyone check, and no name is shown for checking. A
    question nobody works on holds the slot for 30 days.
  - One person with two names, and a result an AI wrote that a person signed without opening
    the link, cannot be told from the record.
  - Three members who use one AI, or read each other's posts first, look like three separate
    checks. The one check by hand in rule 4 is the only guard.
  - Members silent for 90 days stop counting, so a few active members can change the rules.
  - A question anyone could answer without looking can be proposed, answered and closed. It
    counts toward WHEN THIS HAS FAILED and shows nothing. Three yeses are the only gate.
