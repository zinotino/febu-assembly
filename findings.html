5. What a three-job design looks like

A test run with three separate jobs (restate febu's problems, propose mechanisms, check them) passed 15 of 33 proposed mechanisms, rejected 15 and left 3 unsettled. Fourteen of the fifteen rejections had the same flaw: the check compared the group's own words with the group's own words. All 15 survivors share one shape: a ledger, a checker who is not the author, a correction or penalty on breach, and a dated test for when the process itself has failed. Nine of the 15 check claims against public sources (leaders, treaties, terms of service, circulating claims). The other six (mutual aid, skills practice, household readiness twice, two grievance panels) check members' own photos, receipts or filed documents, which the checker named as the weak spot. This is model text grading model text. It sorts designs and shows none of them works. Row P181.

6. What we got wrong along the way

  • Five of twenty written predictions failed, two outright and three in part. They are listed as W1 to W5 in ledger.txt.
  • The news lead was withdrawn and then restored. The general vocabulary does not move at the August restart, but event words double there (P183, P185).
  • "No page was ever held back" was too strong. Page numbers have no gaps, but six time slots after 07-02 were skipped and 22 pages needed a second or third attempt (P184).
  • A false "not found". The first check of the archived site script searched compressed bytes and reported no UBEF text. It was caught and redone (P182).
  • The word counts mixed matching rules. Laugh was given as 51 pages; three of those are "slaughter" and "onslaught", so 48 is right for words that start with the stem. Jest, mock and debt shifted by one or two the same way. The event-word figure of 32 of 74 used eight stems that were never saved; the 14 named stems give 30.
  • Page 133 was overstated. Only page 70 is marked as self-contradiction by both raters.
  • "About zero income since 08-10" was wrong. The first count only caught money that arrived without the wallet signing for it. Reading the creator-fee account directly gives 142 SOL collected between 08-13 and 09-14 (captures/fee_vault_20261013/).

7. Limits

  • The page ratings are a model's reading. The second rater said yes more often: on 71 pages against 37 for demanding belief, and on 113 against 75 for criticising authority.
  • The eleven problem themes and their page counts (section 5) come from one model pass that put each page under a single theme, with no second rater. Most pages touch several themes and the small ones are undercounted: misinformation has 4 pages but at least 4 more fit it, and environment has 3 with at least 1 more (page 102, dwindling water) filed under war. About 8 to 10 of the 124 placements are weak fits.
  • The plain model used for comparison is not the model febu ran on.
  • Failed write attempts cannot be seen. The retry findings are inferred from pages that landed.
  • "Predictions were written before each run" rests on file save times. The test folder is not tracked in git.
