A public list of promises and facts. Nobody approves anything. Anyone can check anything. Nothing is removed except personal information (rule 2). Febu worked the other way: its admin account approved a developer account as winner of the $500 job #004, job #002 was deleted, announcements cost nothing, and the fees went to the wallet that later sold.

THE RULES

1.

The list holds promises ("I will do X by this date; here is how anyone will know") and facts ("X is true; this link shows it"). A forecast of what nobody here does ("the sun will rise tomorrow") is neither; it goes in the chat. Every link must open for anyone, without asking the person who posted it.

2.

Anyone may add to it. Nothing is removed or rewritten: every change is a new dated line. Broken promises and wrong facts stay, marked. Personal information may be replaced by "[removed]".

3.

Nobody checks their own entry. An entry stands, or falls, when two members other than its writer have posted the same result and what they saw at the link. It is kept open, or reopened, the same way: two people, member or not, post the same different result and what they saw. One person's disagreement goes under the entry as a NOTE, and the entry stays as it was. An entry in the group's name has a writer too: the member who proposed it.

4.

An entry kept open or reopened under rule 3 carries both results. After 30 days without closing under rule 3 it is marked CAN'T TELL.

5.

Money moves wallet to wallet, as promises kept. No pool and no treasury. The group makes no new token. The only token used here is $FEBU. A broken promise's bond goes to the incinerator (see Bond). No entry, and no post in the group's name, states or implies a price for $FEBU.

6.

Each week a different member checks both ways: every result posted in the chat that week is in this file, and every SEEN line in this file has its post in the chat. That member adds whatever was posted that week and is not yet in the file, starting from the file whose SHA-256 was last posted, and posts the new file's SHA-256 somewhere the group does not control. The ledger is the file that matches the last posted hash, wherever it is served. A turn not done by the end of its week passes to the next member in the order.

7.

A rule changes, and anything is done in the group's name, only after it has been posted in the chat for 7 days, at least four active members have written "yes" (every active member, if fewer than five are active), and at most one has written "object". The old wording stays. Anyone may copy this list and run it their own way.

8.

Anything only one person can do is a bug. It goes under KNOWN BUGS with a date to fix it by.

WORDS USED HERE

Member

CrypticK, Crypto Fish, Ksizzle, Laura, Merc, WitnesstoFebu, Zurandol. Adding a member works like a rule change. The weekly turn in rule 6 goes in this order. A member who has posted nothing in the chat for 90 days is not active: they do not count toward rules 3, 6 and 7 until they post again.

Bond

the $FEBU a promise names under IF BROKEN. The promiser picks the amount, with no minimum, and a reader weighs the promise by it. A promise may name no bond; then IF BROKEN says "none". A fact never has one: a wrong fact costs its writer the DOESN'T HOLD line. Within 7 days of a promise closing BROKEN, the promiser pays the bond from their own wallet to the incinerator, 1nc1nerator11111111111111111111111111111111, where nobody can spend it, or burns it from that wallet; either way the transaction is the link. No contract holds it in advance: one that did would be the pool rule 5 forbids. Unpaid after 7 days, the entry says UNPAID until it is paid. Whoever owes a bond posts no new promise until it is paid.

Result

HOLDS or DOESN'T HOLD for a fact, KEPT or BROKEN for a promise. "The same result" is the same word on the same WRONG IF lines.

Saw

you opened the link yourself. An AI may help you find something on the page. It never posts a result.

Number

facts and promises share one series, in the order they are posted in the chat.

Hash

the SHA-256 of ledger.txt. Download the file and hash the file; pasting the text into a box can change it. The ledger page shows the hash too.

No totals, no rankings, no scores. What stands under a name is what happened, entry by entry. Nobody judges whether an entry is worth having: one nobody needs goes unchecked and stays OPEN, and ten easy ones add up to nothing.

HOW TO CHECK AN ENTRY

1.

Open the link in the entry. You need no account.

2.

Look for what the entry says is there, before you read anyone else's post about it.

3.

Post in the chat: the entry number, what you saw (the number, the words or the date), which WRONG IF lines you tested, one thing on the page that is not in the entry or in any earlier post about it, and your result. A post that only says HOLDS counts for nothing.

4.

Your post goes under the entry as a SEEN line. Add it yourself or ask any member to.

An entry is OPEN until rule 3 closes it. A reopened entry closes the same way, counting only posts made after it reopened.

HOW AN ENTRY IS WRITTEN

FACT n. (who, date)                     PROMISE n. (who, date)
  SAYS      one sentence                  WILL       what they will do
  LINK      full links you can tap        BY         the date
  WRONG IF  what would show it false      KNOWN BY   the link anyone will open then
  SEEN      results, as they come         IF BROKEN  the bond in $FEBU, or none
                                          SEEN       results, on or after the date

Every part of SAYS needs a WRONG IF line that would catch it. If SAYS claims more, cut SAYS. WRONG IF is written before anyone checks and never changed.
Anything the group, or a member speaking for it, tells people outside the group about what will happen goes here first, as a promise.
A promise can be withdrawn up to 7 days before its date. WITHDRAWN stays here. Withdrawn later, it counts as BROKEN.
A promise to pay names the amount, what it is paid in, and the date. Whoever has not paid by then posts no new promise to pay until they have. The payment itself is a fact: its link is the transaction. A bond is paid the same way, and its payment is a fact too.

Two members who agree in advance can close a false fact until someone else opens the link. Every link is public, so it lasts only until somebody looks.

-

Nobody is paid to check. People check what they want to rely on.

-

Nothing makes anyone look. An entry nobody checks stays OPEN and is cited as open.

-

A bond is paid by the promiser, or not. Nothing holds it in advance. An unpaid bond stays under the name, with BROKEN, and that is the whole enforcement.

-

Two members who agree in advance can close a bonded promise KEPT.

-

Two people who agree in advance can hold any entry open until CAN'T TELL.

-

One person with two names, and a result an AI wrote that a person signed without opening the link, cannot be told from the record.

-

Members silent for 90 days stop counting, so a few active members can change the rules.

-

An entry anyone could get right without looking can be posted and closed. It counts toward PROMISE 7 and shows nothing; it stays under its writer's name for anyone to read.
