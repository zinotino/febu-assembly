WHAT THIS IS
  A public list of promises and facts. Nobody approves anything. Anyone can check anything.
  Nothing is removed except personal information (rule 2). Febu worked the other way: its admin
  account approved a developer account as winner of the $500 job #004, job #002 was deleted,
  announcements cost nothing, and the fees went to the wallet that later sold.

THE RULES
  1. The list holds promises ("I will do X by this date; here is how anyone will know") and
     facts ("X is true; this link shows it"). Every link must open for anyone, without asking
     the person who posted it.
  2. Anyone may add to it. Nothing is removed or rewritten: every change is a new dated line.
     Broken promises and wrong facts stay, marked. Personal information may be replaced by
     "[removed]".
  3. Nobody checks their own entry. An entry stands, or falls, when two members other than its
     writer have posted the same result and what they saw at the link. It is kept open, or
     reopened, the same way: two people, member or not, post the same different result and
     what they saw. One person's disagreement goes under the entry as a NOTE, and the entry
     stays as it was.
  4. An entry kept open or reopened under rule 3 carries both results. After 30 days without
     closing under rule 3 it is marked CAN'T TELL.
  5. Money moves wallet to wallet, as promises kept. No pool and no treasury. The group makes no
     new token. The only token used here is $FEBU. A broken promise's bond goes to the
     incinerator (see Bond). No entry, and no post in the group's name, states or implies a
     price for $FEBU.
  6. Each week a different member checks both ways: every result posted in the chat that week
     is in this file, and every SEEN line in this file has its post in the chat. That member
     adds whatever was posted that week and is not yet in the file, starting from the file
     whose SHA-256 was last posted, and posts the new file's SHA-256 somewhere the group does
     not control. The ledger is the file that matches the last posted hash, wherever it is served.
  7. A rule changes, and anything is done in the group's name, only after it has been posted in
     the chat for 7 days, at least four active members have written "yes" (every active member,
     if fewer than five are active), and at most one has written "object". The old wording
     stays. Anyone may copy this list and run it their own way.
  8. Anything only one person can do is a bug. It goes under KNOWN BUGS with a date to fix it by.

HOW TO CHECK AN ENTRY
  1. Open the link in the entry. You need no account.
  2. Look for what the entry says is there, before you read anyone else's post about it.
  3. Post in the chat: the entry number, what you saw (the number, the words or the date),
     which WRONG IF lines you tested, one thing on the page that is not in the entry or in any
     earlier post about it, and your result. A post that only says HOLDS counts for nothing.
  4. Your post goes under the entry as a SEEN line. Add it yourself or ask any member to.
  An entry is OPEN until rule 3 closes it. A reopened entry closes the same way, counting only
  posts made after it reopened.

HOW AN ENTRY IS WRITTEN
  FACT n. (who, date)                     PROMISE n. (who, date)
    SAYS      one sentence                  WILL       what they will do
    LINK      full links you can tap        BY         the date
    WRONG IF  what would show it false      KNOWN BY   the link anyone will open then
    SEEN      results, as they come         IF BROKEN  the bond in $FEBU, or none
                                            SEEN       results, on or after the date
  Every part of SAYS needs a WRONG IF line that would catch it. If SAYS claims more, cut SAYS.
  WRONG IF is written before anyone checks and never changed.
  Anything the group, or a member speaking for it, tells people outside the group about what
  will happen goes here first, as a promise.
  A promise can be withdrawn up to 7 days before its date. WITHDRAWN stays here. Withdrawn
  later, it counts as BROKEN.
  A promise to pay names the amount, what it is paid in, and the date. Whoever has not paid by
  then posts no new promise to pay until they have. The payment itself is a fact: its link is
  the transaction. A bond is paid the same way, and its payment is a fact too.

RISKS WE ACCEPT
  - Two members who agree in advance can close a false fact until someone else opens the link.
    Every link is public, so it lasts only until somebody looks.
  - Nobody is paid to check. People check what they want to rely on.
  - Nothing makes anyone look. An entry nobody checks stays OPEN and is cited as open.
  - A bond is paid by the promiser, or not. Nothing holds it in advance. An unpaid bond stays
    under the name, with BROKEN, and that is the whole enforcement.
  - Two members who agree in advance can close a bonded promise KEPT.
  - Two people who agree in advance can hold any entry open until CAN'T TELL.
  - One person with two names, and a result an AI wrote that a person signed without opening
    the link, cannot be told from the record.
  - Members silent for 90 days stop counting, so a few active members can change the rules.
